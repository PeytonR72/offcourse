The idea reads as a warm retro space game in which judging other people's everyday preferences determines how far a small rocket travels.

Reviewed every screenshot in `pass-2` (all five routes), `pass-3/home`, `pass-3/daily`, `final-interactions`, and the three `interactions/flight-*.png` frames. Contact sheets were used for complete coverage, with native-size inspection of the homepage, final full aim layouts and mobile keyboard aim. Newer captures supersede older game layouts. Read the capture reports, `final-interactions/metadata.json`, `final-interactions/arrival-samples.json`, and `audit-3.txt`. No implementation, design rationale or earlier critic reports were consulted. Scores are estimates against the supplied rubric, not an award prediction.

The `hover.noHoverChange` entries for Lock in the automated reports concern the visibly disabled control. The supplied enabled-state captures supersede that evidence; I do not count this as a missing primary hover. Similarly, the final mobile card does **not** require separate views for the flight and Lock button once scrolled into position. The homepage's reported lack of idle motion is not inherently a fault: its static composition holds together, and the gameplay reports do record idle motion.

Ranked refinements that would raise the scores:

1. **Bring the first question closer to the initial fold.** `pass-3/daily/m-01-hero.png`, `daily/d-01-hero.png`, and `final-interactions/desktop-aim-full.png`: introduction, a standalone disclosure panel, a standalone score panel and generous gaps precede the flight/question. Compact the introduction/status spacing and integrate the disclosure into a shorter, still-readable band. Preserve the playable card proportions. This would improve the arrival into gameplay, not repair a broken card.
2. **Make the result comparison typographically parallel.** `final-interactions/mobile-result-card.png`: “Your prediction:44% YES” wraps while “Actual YES:34%” stays on one line. Use matching short labels above matching large percentage values in both cells. The earned-distance panel is already clear; the comparison should be equally immediate.
3. **Carry the illustration into the social preview.** `final-interactions/social.png`: almost all of the distinctive rocket/planet personality disappears, leaving centered typography. Add a restrained version of the existing rocket and orbital path without reducing the wordmark's legibility. This strengthens recognition within the existing concept.
4. **Give shared links their route's identity.** `final-interactions/metadata.json`: `/daily`, `/endless` and `/privacy` inherit homepage OG title, description and URL. Mirror their existing unique page metadata into those social fields so a shared game-mode link communicates its actual destination.

No blocking visual or accessibility defect is established by the supplied final evidence. The remaining items are refinements to a coherent professional revamp. This assessment does not certify unseen gameplay states or real-device behavior beyond the captures and reports.
