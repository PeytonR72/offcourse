# Observatory artwork

`observatory.webp` is original background art generated with the built-in image-generation tool for the user-authorized Offcourse design refinement. It is scenery only; typography, controls, and the original rocket sprite are live elements. The generated PNG was encoded as WebP at quality 84 without altering its composition.

`observatory-flight.webp` is the same scenery resized to 1024px wide at WebP
quality 80 for the smaller flight viewport. Game routes preload this derivative
so it does not wait for client hydration before starting its network request.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original background art for a polished indie browser space game called Offcourse. Create a wide 1536x1024 pixel-art space vista with beautifully controlled clusters of crisp pixels, sophisticated 2D game environment painting, subtle dithering and deep atmospheric depth, not blurry and not 3D. Composition: very dark indigo navy negative space across the central 60 percent and entire upper middle for live typography and a separate small rocket sprite. At far left a large cropped peach ochre rocky moon softly lit along its upper right edge. At far right a spectacular mint teal ringed planet with elegant diagonal rings, partly cropped by edge. Bottom edge only: a shadowy navy rocky moon surface and tiny glowing amber observatory windows, silhouetted antenna. Very sparse tiny stars, a faint dusty teal nebula flowing on diagonal behind the planets, a few miniature remote moons. Palette dominated #080f20, #142039, muted #35526a; highlights warm cream #fff0cc, apricot #ffc985, desaturated mint #94d9cb. Center must remain calm and almost empty, quiet and magical with restrained contrast. Strong artistic light and shape design, tactile retro 32-bit game art. No text, no letters, no typography, no logo, no UI, no spaceship, no rocket. This is background scenery not a mockup. Wide landscape framing.
